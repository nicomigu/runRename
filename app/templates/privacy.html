Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

When you connect your Strava account, LogLine accesses:

  • Activity name, type, distance, and duration
  • Start location (latitude/longitude) and start time
  • Lap data (for structured workout detection)
  • Heart rate and effort metrics (when available)

We also store your Strava display name and profile picture for the dashboard.

How we use it

Activity data is used to generate a creative name for your run. We combine it with weather data from Open-Meteo (a free, open weather API) and send a summary to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the new title. The generated name is then written back to your Strava activity.

We do not use your data to train any AI or machine learning models.

Data retention

Raw activity data from Strava (location, laps, metrics) is automatically purged within 7 days of being received. After that, we retain only the generated name, original name, and a timestamp so you can see your rename history on the dashboard.

Your Strava OAuth tokens are stored to maintain your connection and are deleted immediately if you disconnect your account.

Third parties

  • Strava — we read and write activity data via their API
  • Open-Meteo — we fetch weather conditions for your activity's location and time
  • Anthropic (Claude) — we send activity context (not your name or profile) to generate the title

We do not sell, share, or distribute your data to anyone else.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the dashboard. This removes your user record, activity history, preferences, and revokes our access to your Strava account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

You can also revoke access directly from Strava's connected apps settings.
