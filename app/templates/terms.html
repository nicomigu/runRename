What LogLine does

LogLine is a subscription service that automatically renames your Strava activities using AI-generated titles based on weather, time of day, and effort data. By using LogLine you agree to these terms.

Account and access

You sign in via Strava OAuth. You must have a valid Strava account to use LogLine. You are responsible for any activity that occurs through your connected account.

Free beta users receive full access at no cost. Paid subscribers gain access to auto-renaming after purchasing a subscription through Lemon Squeezy.

Subscriptions and billing

Paid subscriptions are billed through Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. By subscribing, you also agree to Lemon Squeezy's Terms of Service.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel anytime from your Lemon Squeezy customer portal — access is provided in your purchase confirmation email.

What we modify on your Strava account

When auto-rename is enabled, LogLine writes a new title to your Strava activities. The original title is stored in your LogLine dashboard so you can see what changed. LogLine does not modify any other aspect of your Strava account or activities.

AI-generated content

Activity titles are generated by an AI language model. While we aim for creative and appropriate names, we cannot guarantee that every generated title will be suitable. You can always manually edit your activity title on Strava.
